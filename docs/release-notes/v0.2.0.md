# NO Missile Indicators v0.2.0

The arrows were too small and too far out to notice in a fight. They now sit on a ring around your HUD and flash red and yellow like the game's missile warning. There's also a Project Wingman style, if you'd rather have its 3D missile indicator.

## What's new

- **Arrows on a ring around the HUD.** **Missile Arrows → Ring size** sets how far out they sit, in percent of the screen height. The default is 30. Set it to 150 to put them back on the screen edge.
- **Flashing arrows.** They flash between **Arrow colour** (red) and the new **Flash colour** (yellow), as fast as the game's own warning.
- **Project Wingman style.** Set **Missile Arrows → Style** to `ProjectWingman`. Every warned missile gets a solid 3D plate on a sphere around you, turned to face it, red on the side facing the missile and yellow behind. A missile behind you shows a red plate below the middle of the screen, one ahead a yellow plate above it, and one beside you a plate at that side.

![Project Wingman style: two red plates for missiles behind, two yellow plates for missiles ahead](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/baanish/NO-Missile-Indicators/main/docs/images/project-wingman-closeup.jpg)

Both styles still show only missiles your missile warning knows about, at the position the game's own HUD marker shows, and both stay off in PvP unless you turn on **Allow in PvP**.

## Upgrading

Your saved settings carry over. The new settings start at their defaults, so the arrows move onto the ring and the style stays Arrows.

## Test status

- 30 automated tests pass, covering arrow and plate placement, known positions, the settings copy, and the PvP rule.
- Checked in game on Nuclear Option 0.34.2 with BepInEx 5.4.23.4, single player in the cockpit view at 1920x1080: the ring, the flashing, and each Project Wingman case (a missile behind, ahead, left, right, and at two o'clock). With six missiles warned at once, the Project Wingman style costs about 0.16 ms a frame, the arrows about 0.01 ms.
- Not checked yet: the third-person view, other resolutions, and a match with a human on the other side.

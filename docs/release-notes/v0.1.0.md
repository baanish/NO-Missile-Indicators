# NO Missile Indicators v0.1.0

A red arrow on the edge of your screen for each incoming missile outside your view, pointing the way to turn, so you can find it in time to defend against it or shoot it down.

The arrows used to ship inside Baanish UI Improvements, now [NO Map Overhaul](https://github.com/baanish/NO-Map-Overhaul). They're a HUD combat aid rather than a map feature, so they now live in their own mod.

## What it does

- **One arrow per incoming missile,** on the correct side of the screen even when the missile is behind you.
- **Only what the game already shows you:** only missiles your missile warning knows about get an arrow. Each arrow follows the game's own HUD marker for its missile, and freezes and fades when the game's marker does.
- **Off in PvP by default:** in a multiplayer game where another human isn't on your faction, including one still picking a side, the arrows are off. **Missile Arrows → Allow in PvP** turns them on there. Against other players the arrows may be a competitive advantage, so only turn it on when everyone knows and agrees. F1 shows whether the arrows are showing right now, and why.

## Upgrading from Baanish UI Improvements

Update that mod to NO Map Overhaul 0.5.0 or later, which no longer draws arrows, so you don't get two arrows per missile. Your arrow settings are copied over on the first start. The [install guide](../INSTALL.md) covers installing and upgrading.
